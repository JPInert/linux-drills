"Out-of-band" here is a service on THIS box that dials out to the NOC's jump host and holds a tunnel open. Find that unit, and read its log: it says why the jump host turns it away.
`systemctl list-units | grep -i tunnel` finds it (it is restarting, not failed, so `systemctl --failed` does not list it); `systemctl status oob-tunnel` shows it restarting every few seconds, and `sudo journalctl -u oob-tunnel | tail -15` (-u = this unit's log) has ssh's own complaint about the private key file.
`sudo ls -l /var/lib/oob/.ssh/` (-l = permissions and owner). ssh ignores a private key that group or others can read: `sudo chmod 600 /var/lib/oob/.ssh/id_ed25519` (600 = owner read+write, nobody else anything), keep the owner `oob`, then `sudo systemctl restart oob-tunnel` and prove it with `sudo ss -tnp | grep 2222` (-t TCP -n numbers -p program) and `nc -w 2 198.51.100.10 22003 < /dev/null` (should print an SSH-2.0 banner).
