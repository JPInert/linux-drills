You are counting things in two text logs. Look at a few raw lines of each first (head -3 FILE) so you know what a failed login and a 5xx line look like, then filter to the RIGHT lines BEFORE you count anything.
Build each answer one | step at a time and look at the output after every step: grep keeps matching lines, grep -o keeps only the matching part, sort + uniq -c counts each value, sort -rn puts the biggest first. In access.log the status is the 6th word on the line; the bytes after it are numbers too.
cd /var/log/nvr, then (1) `grep 'AUTH FAIL' auth.log | grep -o 'ip=[0-9.]*' | sort | uniq -c | sort -rn | head -3` (-o = only the match, -c = count, -rn = biggest number first); (2) `grep '^2026-09-23 02:' access.log | awk '{print $6}' | grep '^5' | wc -l` (^ = start of line, awk '{print $6}' = only word 6, wc -l = count lines); (3) `awk '{print $6, $5}' access.log | grep '^404 ' | grep -o 'cam/[0-9]*' | sort | uniq -c | sort -rn | head -3`.
