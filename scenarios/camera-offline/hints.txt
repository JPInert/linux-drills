Before you blame the camera, prove this box is fine: does it have the right address (compare the install sheet in your home folder), a route out, and a gateway that answers? Then you can say what is NOT broken.
Use `ip a` (look for the "inet" line), `ip r` (the "default via" line is the gateway), then ping the gateway and then the camera. Read exactly WHO replies to the camera ping ("From 10.50.0.254 ...") and what the message says.
Run: ping -c2 10.50.0.254  (-c2 = send 2 pings, then stop)  ;  ping -c2 10.50.1.31  ;  traceroute 10.50.1.31  (shows each hop; !H = "host unreachable" reported by that hop). If the gateway says the camera is unreachable, the fault is past this box: answer "..." naming the device, the next physical check, and that the NVR should NOT be rebooted.
