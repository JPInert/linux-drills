Only in the afternoon, only the detector: think heat, not network. Ask the GPU how hot it is, how fast its fan and clocks run, and whether something is holding it back; then find who set that.
nvidia-smi (the table, then -q -d for the detail sections), the analytics log, the journal to see who changed the card, and systemctl to see what runs at boot.
`nvidia-smi` (Fan / Temp / Pwr:Usage/Cap), `nvidia-smi -q -d PERFORMANCE,POWER` (-q = query details, -d = only these sections; ": Active" = what throttles it, Current vs Default Power Limit), `sudo journalctl -b | grep -i "power limit"` (-b = this boot) finds the unit; `systemctl cat` it, then `sudo systemctl disable` it and undo it live: `sudo nvidia-smi -pl 70` (-pl = power limit, watts) and `sudo gpu-fan --auto`.
