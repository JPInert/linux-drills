A slow box can be short on CPU, memory, disk or network. Ask first: which single program is using the most CPU right now, and is it a service that systemd restarts?
Run `top` (P = sort by CPU, q = quit) for the name, `systemctl cat` it for its Restart= line and `sudo journalctl -u` it for what it is doing. Stop the UNIT, not the PID. Turning it off for good (disable) is the owner's call, not yours: tell them.
Run: top  then: systemctl cat frame-indexer  (Restart=always = a kill won't stick)  then: sudo systemctl stop frame-indexer  (or keep the feature, capped: sudo systemctl set-property frame-indexer CPUQuota=20%  = at most 20% of one CPU). Then write in the ticket what you stopped, the log line, and that it is still enabled.
